# Legal Basis of the Study

This document cross-checks the scope of the present study against the license agreement of the
Erwin Blueberry app (recorded in [`user_agreement.md`](user_agreement.md)) and records the owner's
declaration of non-conflict between the study and the terms of the agreement and applicable law.

## 1. Lawfulness of Ownership and Access

The study's author is the lawful owner of the equipment — an Apex TruCare III insulin pump and a
Xiaomi 11T Pro mobile phone — and has lawfully obtained access to use the Erwin Blueberry program.
On this basis, the author considers himself the lawful owner of the program's copy.

## 2. Applicable Law

Under the jurisdiction chosen by the app's rights holder (clause 12.1 of the agreement), the
Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Russian Federation;
disputes are resolved under the laws of the Russian Federation.

## 3. Intellectual Property (clause 16.1)

Clause 16.1 of the agreement:

> All rights to the Application's source code, interface design, trademarks (Trucare), and the
> patented mathematical algorithms of the "Autopilot" function belong to Erwin Company.

**Declaration.** In the course of this study, the source code of the Erwin Blueberry app and its
patented mathematical algorithms are not studied, and the corresponding rights are not infringed;
rights to the interface design and trademarks are not affected.

## 4. License Restrictions (clause 16.2) and the Study's Subject

Clause 16.2 of the agreement:

> The User is granted a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Application.
> The User is strictly prohibited from decompiling, disassembling, reverse engineering, attempting
> to extract source code, modifying the mathematical dosing models, or creating derivative
> applications based on Erwin Blueberry code.

**Subject and boundaries of the study.** The subject of the study is the data exchange protocol
with the insulin pump belonging to the author: the commands transmitted over the radio link, the
device's responses, and its observed reaction to these commands. The data sources are records of
the exchange between the author's phone and his pump, including HCI logs, and observations of the
pump's operation during its normal use.

The study is limited to external interaction with the device. The source and object code of the
Erwin Blueberry app, its internal workings, the dose-calculation algorithms, and the "Autopilot"
function are not objects of study. Decompilation, disassembly, code extraction, or modification of
the app are not performed within this study. The description of the protocol is compiled based on
the observed commands, responses, and reactions of the pump.

**Right to study the program's functioning.** To the extent that observing the exchange touches
upon the functioning of the Erwin Blueberry app, the author proceeds from the right of a lawful
user to study, investigate, and test the functioning of the program in order to determine the
underlying ideas and principles, as provided for in paragraph 2 of Article 1280 of the Civil Code
of the Russian Federation. This provision is not treated as a right to decompile the app: this
method is not used in the study.

## 5. Final Declaration

The study is limited to external, observable interaction with the pump belonging to the author and
does not include decompilation, disassembly, reverse engineering of the app's code, extraction of
source code, modification of the mathematical dosing models, or the creation of derivative
applications. Thus, the study does not violate clauses 16.1 and 16.2 of the license agreement and
complies with applicable law (clause 12.1 of the agreement; paragraph 2, Article 1280 of the Civil
Code of the Russian Federation).
